EVO Tuning

EVO Tuning unlocks an extra layer of upgrades for select cars. Max out an EVO-eligible car, then collect EVO items to build a unique, more powerful setup.

How EVO Tuning Works

−
  • Unlocks automatically when you fully star-up an EVO-eligible car.
  • Collect EVO items (engine, drivetrain, nitro, etc.) through the EVO Shop and events.
  • Each item has weight and stat boosts; total weight affects handling and performance.
  • Builds can be tailored to track type — e.g. acceleration for short Gauntlet tracks, top speed for long routes.
  • EVO cars are often featured in dedicated EVO Special Events and EVO Ranked competitions.
