Gauntlet Mode

Skill-based 5-race gauntlet across five 30-second track variations. Pick the right cars for short, twisty tracks and optimize your defense lineups.

Gauntlet Rules

−
  • Unlock: 70 Career Flags and at least 5 owned cars.
  • Defense: Set 5 cars for 5 consecutive 30″ track variations (one car per track).
  • Challenge: Race against 1 of 3 opponents; win 3 of 5 races to win the challenge.
  • Leagues / Divisions: Matchmaking uses the combined rank of your top 5 owned cars.
  • Refresh cost: 25,000 credits initially, increasing up to 200,000.
  • Strategy: Short tracks favor acceleration, handling, and nitro over raw top speed.
